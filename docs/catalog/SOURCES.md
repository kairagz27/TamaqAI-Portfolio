# Current catalogue sources

This snapshot distribution contains only **39 attributed Open Food Facts entries**, retrieved and qualified on 20 October 2026. See [data sources](../DATA_SOURCES.md) for license conditions, dates, qualification rules and limitations. The selected source evidence is retained in [the evidence JSON](openfoodfacts-evidence-20261020.json).

Country tags and values are community data, not verified current shelf stock or measured portions. Each retained record preserves its source URL, basis, check date and barcode qualification. Unknown nutrients remain unknown; they must not silently become zero in daily totals.

Manufacturer and restaurant datasets with unconfirmed redistribution terms are not included. No verified complete Coffee Boom nutrition dataset is claimed. Product/brand names identify source records and do not imply endorsement.

The seed is licensed separately under ODbL 1.0 / DbCL 1.0, with attribution. It contains no source product photographs or packaging artwork. [Import behavior](IMPORT.md) preserves confirmed diary snapshots and skips unchanged versions.
