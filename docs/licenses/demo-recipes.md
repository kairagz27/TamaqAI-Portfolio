# Demonstration recipes

The six recipes in `DemoRecipes.kt` are original, synthetic TamaqAI fixtures,
distributed under the project's MIT license. They were written for this feature;
no recipe website, restaurant menu, paid source or third-party recipe dataset
was copied. Translations and ingredient amounts are part of the same fixtures.

Nutrition values are illustrative estimates, not manufacturer measurements or
dietary advice. The vegetable soup intentionally has an ingredient with unknown
quantity and nutrients to demonstrate incomplete totals. The UI identifies these
recipes as demonstration data; they enter a diary only after explicit confirmation.

Ingredient identifiers are canonical only within this small set. Raw and cooked
rice have distinct preparation states. Grams, millilitres and pieces do not imply
a density or a unit weight; conversion requires explicit, consistent metadata.

This set is separate from the Open Food Facts catalogue and does not change its
ODbL/DbCL attribution or redistribute product photographs.
