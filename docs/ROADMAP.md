# Roadmap and limitations

## Public snapshot

The owner authorized public publication on 23 October 2026. The prepared [release](https://github.com/kairagz27/TamaqAI-Portfolio/releases/tag/v1.3.1-portfolio.1) remains bound to tested commit `b67f95bcdd59a59923e7364df662b91b2b2b463b`; subsequent publication documentation is separate. See the [export audit](export/README.md), [preparation verification](PUBLICATION_VERIFICATION.md) and [publication checks](PUBLICATION.md). The original development repository stays private. Publication does not establish production readiness.

## Before a hosted pilot

Choose an owner-controlled HTTPS endpoint and persistent PostgreSQL/files storage; configure a real sender and verify external delivery. Exercise backup/restore, data-preserving migration/rollback and physical-device account/photo scenarios. Preserve the ledger and spending limits. Do not provision paid resources automatically.

## Follow-up

- Reproduce and resolve the intermittent full-demo Espresso idle timeout around add-sheet/catalogue navigation and the secondary Compose Activity-teardown error. Preserve safe diagnostics and reject failed captures. A successful repeat is not proof of a fix.
- Measure optimized navigation/scroll/camera on physical devices and investigate frame gaps from traces; emulator success does not establish Xiaomi 15 smoothness.
- Expand the small Kazakhstan catalogue only with verified identity, units, sources and redistribution terms; no invented venue nutrition.
- Improve screen/repository boundaries incrementally where actual coupling causes defects.
- Extend accessibility/device coverage and monitoring without personal-data logs.
- Resolve plural/private-resource lint warnings and review dependency updates with regression checks.
- Consider a separately scoped standalone demonstration. The current APK requires the local backend on port 8100 and adb reverse.

Cloud diary sync, national product coverage and measured calorie accuracy are not current promises. Quantitative accuracy evaluation needs weighed portions and reliable recipes. No paid AI evaluation is part of this export.
